CREC-72 GTE complete-build runbook

Purpose and boundary

  This is the one reviewed path for the future paid complete build. CREC-72
  does not authorize a reservation, a Vast instance, or any other paid action.
  Stop at the paid boundary. Continue only after the user approves the exact
  refreshed offer and reservation.

  This extends the calibrated representation. It does not rebuild the image or
  create a different representation.

Cost contract

  maximum rental rate: 1.359260 USD/hour
  rental rate at most 1.359260 USD/hour
  artifact_storage_and_transfer_micro_usd: 10000000
  remaining computation: 36676185 micro-USD
  one cold restart: 148419 micro-USD
  one lost shard: 173723 micro-USD
  additional projection: 46998327 micro-USD
  reservation ceiling after cent rounding: 47.00 USD

  The $10 allowance covers the exact image, 2.68 GB transport, 30.46 GB model
  download, 50.74 GB result retrieval, storage, preparation, and teardown. At
  the admitted $0.02/GB network ceilings, both directions cost at most
  $1.738812. It is deliberately conservative; unused money is not spent.
