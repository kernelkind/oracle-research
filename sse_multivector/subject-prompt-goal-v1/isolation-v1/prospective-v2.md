## Shared review boundary

Apply the existing prompt rules and frozen isolation criteria. Accept equivalent
wording, including passive action descriptions. Record **source**, **copy** and
**scope** separately, with actual numbered witnesses for a failure:

- Source: retain the expressed specific participants, complete shared subjects,
  full and partial actions, amounts and supported qualifications in `subject`
  strings. A complete shared action may also convey the expressed group; an
  additional non-action group heading is not mandatory. Do not demand arbitrary
  combinations across separate topics.
- Copy: inspect every actually returned compatible numbered parent occurrence.
  Its descendants must retain the complete compatible source subjects; a correct
  other branch or `context` cannot repair an omission. Match semantic meaning,
  rather than exact heading text. Missing source meaning and independent copy
  omissions are different findings.
- Scope: each displayed child heading must itself satisfy every restriction of
  its parent. Generic materials, actions or equipment do not acquire a purpose,
  origin, attribute or relationship from surrounding branches or source context.
  Record actual parent exposure; absence of a parent is not a repaired edge.

There is no required exact tree, optional broader-heading closure or exhaustive
taxonomy. Do not infer calculated totals, URL-body subjects, literal meanings of
names or relationships from co-mention. Disputed ingredient membership, narrative
origin and indirect purchase attachment are excluded; P201 supplies its origin
explicitly. If a material interpretation remains disputed, mark uncertainty
instead of inventing gold. Prior scores and calibration remain unchanged.
