the counted ingredient participants and group, every compatible numbered copy,
and unrestricted salt/recipient-only tissue outside food-purpose headings.

six other examples, changing only C1's explanation to add its collection table.

source-resolution instruction and partial-action example. Neither the combined
control nor the table has established reliable model behavior. Existing
development inputs, bounded agent rubrics and small samples limit conclusions;
offline example consistency is not independent human gold or prospective
verification.

attempts finish. The
[matched comparison](../isolation-v1/collection-contrast-v1.md) freezes its 24
native plans with zero reuse before inference. All 107 relevant checks pass.

authoring this control makes no teacher requests and selects no candidate.

the source and its exact POST template. The versioned

[source](ASv6.92.md.revision.json) and

trial 2. Every full and partial action survives. Two source-qualification and
