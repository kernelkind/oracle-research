## Limits retained

These are topical refinements, not one uniform taxonomic relation: category membership, methods, and means of achieving an objective are all present. Domain and relation type are not fully crossed. Some paraphrases supply more explanation or shared vocabulary. Comparably broad alternatives do not eliminate length, generality, or ordinary-similarity shortcuts; the fixed analysis includes those baselines. Political or relationship oppositions need not occupy separate browsing subjects. The question here is whether the candidate actually encompasses this narrower description, not whether it is semantically related.

Thirty case families are not sixty independent samples. No result will establish performance on every corpus domain, fragmented masks, automatically generated labels, or whole hierarchies. Unexpected scores are not sufficient to declare a semantic judgment correct or incorrect.
