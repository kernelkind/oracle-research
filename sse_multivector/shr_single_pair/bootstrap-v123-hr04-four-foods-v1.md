# Bootstrap-v123: qualifying kinds before relationship conditions

## Status and controlled change

The user authorized targeting the qualifying-kind gap exposed by Compare's
counterexamples. [Bootstrap-v123](Hierarchy-bootstrap-v123.md) replaces one
paragraph in `Identify category ambiguities`, directly from tested v122. The
[native edits](hierarchy-bootstrap-v123-qualifying-kind.edits.json) and
[revision receipt](Hierarchy-bootstrap-v123.md.revision.json) preserve exact
before/after text and hashes. Existing Resolve and Write instructions, both
synthetic examples, input shape and output schema wording remain unchanged.
V123 is tested experimentally, not activated or comprehensively qualified.

The revised paragraph first establishes each covered kind and its defining
features, including a defining role, before allowing a use or component
relationship to confer membership. It also asks for material ambiguities in
each proposed condition, including whom it applies to. This does not prescribe
human consumers, edibility, nourishment, universal Food equivalence or blanket
rejection of every medicinal or ceremonial witness.

Two independent read-only draft reviews found no material contradiction with
role-defined kinds, named alternatives, whole-person topics or factual unknowns.
That assessment is not evidence of correct model behavior. Official
[reasoning guidance](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/reasoning-best-practices#how-to-prompt-reasoning-models-effectively)
supports simple, direct instructions and clear delimiters; it does not certify
this particular semantic rule. The edit replaces an existing block rather than
adding an exception or a validation-data example.

## First-draw findings against the actual Compare witnesses

| Case | New conditions | Counterexample consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Risotto | Edible material intended for human consumption; includes dishes and culinary ingredients | Non-edible poison is excluded. The explicitly edible clinical wafer and edible gold swallowed for ceremony remain admitted; no consumption purpose is required. |
| Sourdough | Edible material intended for nourishment or "as part of eating", directly or as an ingredient | Non-edible poison is excluded. The explicitly eaten clinical wafer fits the literal eating alternative. No human condition is stated; animal-feed and swallowing cases retain qualifications. |
| Pie | Edible material intended for human consumption as nourishment | The specified solely diagnostic, medicinal and ceremonial purposes are excluded; animal-only feed and unintended amoeba consumption fail stated human/intention conditions. |
| Brisket | Material intended for human consumption as nourishment | Same purpose/consumer consequences for these witnesses. Its definition does not separately require edibility; no claim of universal poison exclusion is made. |

Diagnostic barium drinks and medicinal mineral oil cannot automatically be
excluded from the risotto meaning merely because they are medical. Their
edibility is not established by those saved hypotheses. They remain conditional
under the new shared edible condition. Conversely, the diagnostic wafer
expressly is edible and intended to be eaten; it cannot be excluded by adding
an unstated culinary or nourishment requirement.

The risotto head now says:

> Food is edible material intended for human consumption

The qualifying edible condition applies to every admitted kind and closes the
specific non-edible ingestion gap observed under V122. Later culinary examples
still cannot narrow this head to culinary-only purposes. Whether that broader
edible-ingestion boundary is acceptable remains a semantic qualification;
admission of every clinical or ceremonial item is not predefined failure gold.

Both reviewers find no definite factual contradiction in the source examples
and no concrete loss of a named ingredient. Purpose and consumer conditions
still enter the outputs without direct ambiguity questions about their
material alternatives. Thus v123 has not demonstrated general closure of the
boundary-identification problem. Sourdough's "as part of eating" is particularly
weak if intended to mean a culinary purpose rather than the act of eating.

## Disputed repeats and fixed-meaning discipline

Risotto's repeat begins:

> Food is edible material intended for human consumption as nourishment or refreshment.

Its subsequent "It includes" sentence refers to this defined category; it does
not independently erase the stated nourishment/refreshment condition. The
specified solely diagnostic, solely ceremonial and solely medicinal witnesses
do not establish those purposes. Medical benefit must not silently become
refreshment. The phrase may still need qualification at a future counterpart.
No concrete lost source ingredient is established; a requirement that each
ingredient independently provide nourishment must not be invented.

Sourdough's repeat retains edible substances intended for nourishment or
"as part of eating" and includes food-preparation ingredients. The edible
clinical wafer remains admitted under the ordinary literal eating condition.
Consumer-relative edibility, drinking and swallowing remain conditional cases.
The actual unintended amoeba episode fails the required intention.

Non-edible poison is therefore excluded in both independent risotto draws and
both sourdough draws. This is a bounded improvement, not a reliability estimate
or proof that the wording caused it. The changed risotto purpose boundary is
variation between independently produced meanings; it is not a contradiction
between comparisons of the same immutable meaning. No draw is spliced or
silently repaired, and no new Food-pair labels are asserted.

The repeat again supplies only broad-versus-example and ingredient-coverage
questions. It does not directly examine the introduced nourishment,
refreshment, eating or consumer conditions. The next proposed focus is to
understand that omission and clarify whether the wider edible-ingestion scope
is deliberately chosen, before appending another rule or prescribing a Food
boundary. No further edit or larger hierarchy trial is implied by this record.
