## Input and review contract

All current subject inputs must originate from the approved ASv6.100 template,
SHA-256 `0e3fdfb713128233ba65070c0092914a38b0059a2eb5e873c0d828add1c5d52f`.
Verify native producer receipts, not merely a version label. Earlier AS data and
downstream results remain historical evidence. Historical user decisions guide
review but do not predetermine the meanings of changed ASv6.100 entries.

Input schema 4 supplies the full original post, the complete original tree under
the selected entry's root, and its selected number. There are no generated
parent meanings, expected labels or preferred outputs in the model input.
The post supplies factual evidence; the selected subject, contexts and original
tree guide category interpretation. Output schema 79 supplies ambiguities,
resolutions, a nullable final meaning and exact contiguous evidence quotes.

Review final meanings for supported identity, abstraction and explicit material
boundaries. General categories are broader than their illustrated instances.
Preserve required roles, purposes, states and defining actions without inventing
facts about a particular occurrence. Diagnostic wording alone does not fail a
clear final meaning. Assess independently generated children against the exact
independently generated ancestors from their own draw; do not broaden a parent
during review or splice preferred endpoints across draws. Joint descriptions
and independently admitted alternatives require different inclusion checks.
Nulls remain unresolved producer coverage, not negative comparison labels.

When alternative scope interpretations change containment, leaving that choice
implicit is a Bootstrap failure. The final meaning must express the chosen
boundary. This does not predetermine a narrow or broad choice, or establish
unknown facts about a particular consumer or occurrence.
