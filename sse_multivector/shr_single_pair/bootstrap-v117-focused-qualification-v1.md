## Pre-call review criteria and gates

Review final meanings from their own original input before using diagnostic
resolutions to interpret them. Then separately check final/diagnostic alignment.
The reviewers know the task rules and historical mechanisms; this is not a
claim of blind experimental-condition assignment or human semantic gold.

- Preserve named identity, expressed restrictions and every named alternative
  or jointly required item. A design/method restriction must define the chosen
  topic, rather than become universal because it appears in one example.
- Risotto preparation must retain making risotto; stovetop/oven branches must
  retain their method. Do not invent eating, plating or a universal recipe.
- Mental states must remain broader than its illustrated states. Do not invent
  medical diagnoses or causes.
- Preserve steel/brass alternatives without deciding which material a particular
  item has. Preserve both the cocotte and frying pan in the complete collection.
- Preserve soup-mug identity through its tea use and distinguish the author's
  mistaken plant-impression belief from the manufacturing process actually
  stated in the post.
- Preserve unknown Coffee reference/affiliation and the distinction between
  a justified category choice and an unsupported instance fact. Ambiguity alone
  does not prescribe null. Tomatoes planned for purchase are not invented as
  part of today's dinner; Champs is a form of address, not proof of membership.
- Challenge each chosen Food definition's conditions across all admitted kinds.
  The accepted old C003 poison witness is historical evidence, not a forced
  label for a fresh meaning. No universal consumer/nourishment boundary is gold.
- Check independently generated selected children against every selected
  ancestor from the same version and first draw. Do not splice favorable draws,
  broaden an ancestor during review or delete an alternative to force a link.
  Report supported source-link conflicts and convention-dependent links
  separately from unsupported or ambiguous producer meanings.
- Valid output, individual meaning quality, branch compatibility and human
  approval are separate. Failed/unrun/null cases stay visible. Empty diagnostics
  are legitimate when no material ambiguity remains; extra nulls are not a fix.

A consequential unsupported fact, lost subject restriction or materially
ambiguous final boundary triggers a fresh repeat of its exact input before
another edit. A reproduced failure warrants a controlled mechanism change and
contrasting controls; do not claim a uniquely responsible sentence from this
whole-prompt comparison. If the focused cohort has no definite failure, proceed
with the candidate's remaining full inventory while preserving these exact
first-draw outputs. No hierarchy build is part of that gate.
