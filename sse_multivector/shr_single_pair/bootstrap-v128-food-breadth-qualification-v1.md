Native agent reviews preserve these qualifications: four uncertain ingredient/

## Instruction/example limit and recommendation

The unchanged Feedback example defines views/evaluations/suggestions **about an
activity or experience**, whereas complete BBv7 removes that subject-range
restriction and includes the defining response relationship. The retained
example can teach a restriction that the new breadth preference asks the model
to reconsider. This is a possible instruction/example tension, not evidence that
it caused a particular Food output. Preserving examples isolates the added
preference but limits attribution; this experiment does not test full BBv7.
The draft's necessity criterion can also interact with original ancestor-supported
role/material scope. Four Food roots cannot qualify that broader consequence.

The first draw shows conditional widening, but the brisket repeat does not
preserve it. No reliable general broadening or resolution of component-purpose
ambiguity is established. Keep v128 as experimental evidence. The proposed next
test is the complete unchanged BBv7 on these same four inputs and counterexamples,
separately labeled from this partial port, before concluding whether the broader
approach helps. A successful breadth condition must preserve defining features,
legitimate ingredients and explicit boundaries; equal output strings or blanket
clinical exclusion are not the objective. This follow-on test has not begun.
