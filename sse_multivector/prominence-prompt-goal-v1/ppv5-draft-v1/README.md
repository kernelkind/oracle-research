# PPv5 — terminology and incidental-focus wording

Status: proposed draft for delta review, not model-tested, frozen or adopted.

Changes from PPv4 are limited to:

- Consistently referring to the subject represented by the mask throughout the
  interpretation instructions, focus assessment and score definitions.
- Removing "brief" as a reason for assigning an incidental-focus score.

The input, output, numerical score bands and remaining instructions are
unchanged. The [PPv3 rationale and limitations](../ppv3-draft-v1/README.md) still
apply. Official OpenAI guidance supports straightforward instructions; it does
not establish that this wording or score scale works.

`PPv5.md.revision.json` records the exact edits and source/output hashes from
the offline revision tool. PPv4 and frozen PPv2 remain untouched. No model calls
or production changes accompany this proposal.

Next suggested step after wording approval: prepare a small comparison of saved
notes about the same subject, without regenerating subjects or masks, to test
whether prominence would order the more directly related notes first.
