# M08 complete subtree audit A

## Scope and evidence

This report covers the 32 assigned disjoint raw inspection roots, not a proposed cut or the complete hierarchy: **437 canonical vectors, 448 original-ID masks, 79 source notes, 1,231 exact fragments and 405 internal transitions**. All 448 masks, including each original alias's own fragment array, were freshly read. Every fragment was checked against its original UTF-8 byte span; fragment/span lengths and all 1,231 byte slices matched.

Complete originals for N007, N013, N023, N030, N034, N041, N048, N059, N069, N071, N072, N073, N078, N080, N088, N092, N098 and N100 were reopened this pass. The other 61 represented originals use the complete-source context already read in the preceding frozen-corpus audits; this is context reuse, not a claim that those 61 were freshly reread. The actual baseline, request, map and notes SHA-256 hashes match M08 and M04/M06/M07. In particular the unchanged map is 8c2a3eebf84855cc676b3a5812aa53401ec9ddf66292139daa7a4ee17a574712 and notes are 9de47b1a66867e612aaf706da5002d4b04b4e9d8192e3782330a693dd1dea019.

All 405 internal child transitions were inspected bottom-up. **148 transitions** have exact recursive matches in fully audited M04, M06 or M07, contained in **89 maximal reusable subtrees**; **257 transitions** have changed structure and are individually classified below. Exact reuse requires the complete alias set at each leaf and recursively unordered child signatures. Matching top memberships is insufficient. There are 110 exact matches to M04, 105 to M06 and 93 to M07 separately; these overlap and must not be added. The registry cites one matching run per reused transition, preferring M07, then M06, then M04. Inherited weak links remain weak; reuse is not a favorable semantic rating.

Within these partitions, 197 internal nodes are displayed and 208 are raw-only under the current complete-note-set collapse. Roots /1204 and /1641 are raw-only; their first displayed ancestors are /1272 and /1652, outside this report. The other 30 inspection roots are displayed. The partition arithmetic is 437 - 32 = 405, and 148 + 257 = 405. The root and transition registries below preserve all nesting rather than flattening each partition into one claimed subject.

M08 declares conditional PARIS on frozen linear affinities: excluded edges contribute neither observed nor expected weight, eligible expected products retain the original endpoint degrees, and the score is (W/Z) times the eligible degree-product sum divided by cross-group weight. This semantic audit did not rerun the candidate or infer numerical causes from word overlap. It created no masks, geometry, algorithms, cuts or generated labels. Generated subjects/titles, source URLs, linked images and external page contents were not semantic evidence. Original health and dietary claims are reported without endorsement.

## Findings within the assigned scope

The result is mixed. Directly readable descendants include /1506's onions across different preparations, /1391's shrimp-containing dishes, /1461's broad egg material, /1518's coffee-taste motivations, /1591's cooking equipment and /1415's food preservation. Broader parents concerning food practices, preparation, personal preferences or household equipment can be defensible without a single shared technique. Conversely, generic numbers, bare tags and unrelated selected fragments still yield weak small combinations, and some higher parents retain those weaknesses.

No variant reunion is claimed as a note-discovery gain or loss. A useful child can already exist through different masks of the same notes in another run. The separately owned counterpart audit is needed for that comparison. All observations below concern the actual selected fragments and nested transitions in this scope.

## Complete mask and transition registry

Every original-ID mask and every internal transition in the assigned partitions appears once below. Each mask line records its source note, canonical leaf index and exact fragment array; aliases have separate lines so differing fragments are not hidden. Numeric internal children refer to the saved M08 tree; slash-separated V IDs denote aliases of one canonical leaf.

Transition classifications describe the new union, not entire notes or proposed cuts:

- D: directly supported shared ingredient, action, functional relation or explicit subject.
- B: defensible broader or qualified connection; the observations retain differences and incidental compound content.
- W: weak or mixed link at this transition; not a rejection of coherent descendants, a ban on broad subjects, or a note-discovery-loss claim.
- R M04/id, R M06/id or R M07/id: exact recursively reused structure, with masks and child placement inspected here; not a positive quality rating.

The 257 changed transitions comprise 96 D, 106 B and 55 W judgments. These are coverage-accounting categories, not an algorithm quality score. The registry also gives the current display/raw-only flag for each internal node. A raw-only node's content remains within retained ancestors; raw-only does not mean its vectors disappear.
