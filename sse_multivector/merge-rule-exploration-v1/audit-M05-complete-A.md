These are provisional semantic judgments, not approved groups. The twenty roots below are disjoint reading batches, not a proposed cut or a set of generated titles. All fragments, including alias variants, were read and matched against their original UTF-8 byte ranges.

Of those 291 merges, 170 have exactly the same recursive child structure as an already-inspected M04 subtree. The other 121 were read bottom-up, with both actual children identified at each transition. Reuse requires equal canonical leaves and equal unordered recursive children—not merely the same final membership. M04's full audit supplies the earlier inspection; no old manual node-retention recommendation is reused.

Near-root breadth remains a real limitation of the unchanged merge-plus-display pipeline.

The reader should distinguish three observations: a clear small child can survive; its next parent can be much broader; and another useful path for a source note can exist elsewhere. This audit does not convert those observations into one semantic pass rate or an overall winner.
