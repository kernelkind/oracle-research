## A real note-access distinction: the three-note tea extension

M04 displayed **1587** contains exactly:

- V333/N042 `["I want", "hot tea too"]` and alias V334 `["I", "want", "hot tea", "too"]`.
- V670/N072 `["every Southerner has a different way of making sweet tea"]`.
- V730/N080 `["got me out of bed", "plus the need for tea and a wee"]`; V731 `["morning", "tea", "wee", "in that order"]`.

Hot tea, morning tea and differing sweet-tea methods share a legitimate broad tea subject. They need not describe the same temperature, recipe or action. N072's full original uses the tea statement as an analogy for soda-bread recipe variation; that does not erase the explicit statement about tea.

M05 preserves **1208**, the exact N042/N080 hot/morning-tea child, with four original IDs/three canonical vectors/two notes. It enters1601's morning activities and then1672. N072's V670 instead enters **1631**, alongside bread/recipe and the quantity quartet. Its other genuinely relevant tea-bearing mask is V671 `["Soda bread", "recipes vary", "I hear", "much like", "Southerner", "different way of making sweet tea"]`, already in1463's bread-related branch.

Allowing **every tea-bearing alternative in those three notes**—N042 V333/V334, N072 V670/V671, N080 V730/V731—the first joint M05 node is raw **1824**, containing818 canonical vectors/844 original IDs/all100 notes. The unchanged display collapses it into root **1830**. M01's first displayed counterpart under this same generous check is1575 with90 notes; M04's1587 is much more focused.

This is a qualified loss of M04's useful three-note extension, **not** loss of all tea discovery or a requirement to reunite all duplicates. The small two-note1208 relation survives, and N072 is meaningfully available through bread. Neither a generic food mask nor an unrelated tag from N072 would replace its actual tea statement. This targeted alternate-mask check is the only new cross-run loss claim established by this upper pass.

## Limits and consequence

A/B plus this report account for every mask and every raw M05 merge under direct reading or exact recursive M04 reuse. This is complete reading coverage, not an exhaustive search for every valid interpretation or every alternative route in every comparator. The earlier [relation audit](audit-M05-relations.md) and [new-group audit](audit-M05-new-groups.md) supply their own bounded evidence; their duplicate-mask and alternate-path qualifications remain necessary.

Broad cooking, food practices, family activity and cultural interests are not automatically poor connections. Equally, an inherited ambiguous mask does not acquire missing source meaning through a broader parent. Opposed ethical/dietary claims remain relevant. No numerical shape, child size, merge score or exact-tree match was treated as a semantic score or proof of a causal mechanism.

Only this report was written. No code, source inputs, masks, vectors, labels, inference runs, tests, Cargo operations, commits, cards or other reports were changed by this inspection.
