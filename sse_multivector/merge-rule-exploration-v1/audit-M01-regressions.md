# M01 groupings versus M04: targeted regression search

## Result and boundary

M04 does not preserve every useful small discovery available in M01. The clearest examples found here are the Christmas connection at M01/1655 and the photography/painting connection at M01/1637. The latter is also present in the baseline. M01/1434 and /1357 provide additional credible, broader connections. These findings do not establish that M01 is better overall, or that the corresponding M04 branches have no useful descendants.

This was a source-grounded regression search, not automatic scoring. The complete raw trees were reconstructed from frozen aliases and merges. All 915 M01 internal memberships were available to the structural comparison. Of these, 302 changed groups have 2–30 notes; 126 changed note-set transitions lack an exactly equal note set anywhere in M04. These counts located candidates only. They are not counts of lost discoveries.

Exact fragments were read for 27 selected M01 groups, covering 205 distinct original vector IDs from 54 notes. The smaller /1637 group was also independently checked inside /1742. Eight candidate subtrees had their complete internal merge sequences examined. This is not complete semantic inspection of all 915 merges: the structural screen is complete, but source-grounded judgment is targeted. No generated group titles, new inference, manual reassignment, or changed inputs were used.

Numbers below count original vector IDs, including aliases, unless explicitly identified as canonical counts. A smallest M04 group containing all named notes was searched across **every mask from those notes**, not just the original M01 vectors. That supplies a structural lower bound. Actual relevant alternate masks were then checked before the strongest loss claims. A group containing a note only through `GM` is not a replacement for that note's photography connection.

## M01/1434: a compact ahead-of-time food-preparation connection

M01/1039 pairs two masks, then /1434 adds the third:

- V095/N013 `["chicken stock", "We save carcasses", "a couple of times a year do this"]`.
- V823/N091 `["Century eggs", "preserving duck, chicken, or quail eggs", "for several weeks to several months"]`.
- V737/N080 `["I soft boiled nine eggs for my lunch over the next few days"]`.

These are different operations and timescales, but all concern preparing or preserving animal foods over time for use beyond an immediate meal. N013's original explicitly says the stock will be pressure canned and stored in the pantry. N080 is short-term preparation for future lunches, not a claim that soft boiling is the same preservation method as making century eggs. This distinction is visible in the fragments rather than inferred from the presence of numbers alone.

All masks and originals from the three notes were read. M04/1029 preserves the N013/N091 pair exactly, as does baseline/1055. M04/1360 instead places V737 with V119/N016's long hunger/laziness/eggs/chocolate/sickness mask and V173/N024's multi-clause red-flags joke about workout plans, non-expiring food, and dislike of ice cream. The egg connection to N016 is real. M04 later joins this to dietary quantities/concerns; the smallest M04 node containing **any** masks from all three relevant notes is /1711 (18 IDs/nine notes).

The alternative N013 masks explicitly mentioning pressure canning and pantry storage were checked: V096, V097, V099–V102. N080's alternative egg masks describe hens, egg supply, breakfast, or the boiled-egg/crisps comparison; they do not restore the three-note preparation-over-time group elsewhere. All N091 masks were checked as well. Thus M04 preserves a useful two-note preservation descendant, but not M01's small three-note extension. Baseline's smallest any-mask three-note counterpart is /1187 (91 IDs/41 notes).

Assessment: credible modest regression in broad-to-narrow navigation, not loss of all egg discovery and not a demand that preservation periods or methods agree.

All five originals and all their alternate masks were read. M04/1339 preserves N013/N048 and adds N048's fuller V372 quotation. Assessment: a credible useful broad grouping missing in M04, with substantial partial preservation and an alternative useful N055 extension. It would be wrong to describe the entire M04 meat branch as a regression, or to claim the five notes share one preparation method.
