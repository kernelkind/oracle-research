# M07: thirteen relation checks with retained alternatives

M07 preserves meaningful Christmas, tea and physical-activity access, restores the three-note photography extension missing from M04–M06, and restores restaurant-availability access with a relevant coffee-shop restriction added. These are specific relation findings, not evidence that PARIS is globally better.

The result is mixed elsewhere. Slaughter has a defensible broader animal-processing counterpart. Food over time remains accessible with a partly weak surround. Preservation still contains the vacuum-sealer/grab-bar weakness. The former five-note animal-flesh group is less focused; food/emotion, seasoning and tomatoes no longer have M06's focused complete extensions under the same generous alternatives. Useful partial relations remain. Getting-going has a compact but differently emphasized sleep/time/routine alternative, with a weak initial join.

## Scope, provenance and method

This pass checks the nine relations and all relevant alternatives explicitly enumerated in [audit-M06-relations.md](audit-M06-relations.md), plus the four strongest candidates in [audit-M06-new-groups.md](audit-M06-new-groups.md): M06/1736, /1442, /1397 and /1285. Those relation names are existing audit questions, not generated group titles.

Freshly read all exact masks and every nested child transition in the 13 minimum displayed M07 counterparts, plus seven small surviving alternatives. The unique complete-subtree coverage is **196 canonical vectors, 202 original IDs, 631 fragments and 177 internal transitions**. Reading every admitted alternative adds 114 original IDs outside those subtrees, for **316 unique original-ID masks / 960 fragments**. Every fragment matches its original UTF-8 byte range. Read all **63 complete source originals** represented by those masks. No linked article, image, thread or video was opened or supplied as evidence.

All M07 counterpart subtrees were read, including the complete 69-canonical tomato counterpart and 61-canonical food/emotion counterpart. Previous-run large ancestors were searched structurally for the declared alternatives; this report does not claim a fresh whole-subtree semantic reading of those large prior ancestors. Previous semantic qualifications come from the two cited full reports, with the relevant masks and originals rechecked here.

Loaded HDBv3's saved single-linkage tree and M01/M04/M05/M06/M07. Alias groups agree in order across them. The four saved source records agree across these experiment files, and all four M07 input files hash to their recorded values. Frozen inputs remain 916 canonical vectors / 945 original IDs / 100 notes. M07's method is `paris_linear_eligible_graph`, not source-note-pair weighting.

For each required note, a candidate must contain at least one admitted relevant mask. Among qualifying raw nodes, minimize distinct notes, then canonical size, then node ID; repeat for displayed nodes. Display keeps the root and raw multi-note groups whose complete note set differs from their immediate raw parent's. Thus a raw-to-display replacement does not remove its masks or full-note access. Reconstructed display counts are HDBv3 246, M01 428, M04 485, M05 500, M06 488 and M07 904; these are not semantic scores or an independent interactive viewer check.

No new alternatives were introduced. The previously specified broad restaurant check and N022/V152 quantity alternative were retained; both leave all counterpart results below unchanged. Compound masks, opposite attitudes and tag-supported relations remain admissible. Unrelated text elsewhere in a full note is not inserted into a selected mask's meaning.
