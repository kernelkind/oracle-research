# M05: bounded recheck of seven previously identified relations

These are provisional source-grounded judgments, not user approvals or a complete semantic audit. M05 preserves the useful M04 slaughter, food/emotion, and preservation relations. It does not recover M01's useful Christmas extension, photography extension, or five-note animal-flesh grouping. Food prepared or kept for later use requires a qualified comparison: the explicit future-lunch route is separated, but another relevant storage-related route remains.

## Method and coverage

Sources are `M05.json`, `M01.json`, `M04.json`, `../real-note-fine-segmentation-v1/vectors/vector-map.json`, and `../real-note-fine-segmentation-v1/reviewed-input.json`, with the prior reports as comparison guides. M05's saved definition is the ordinary median of eligible cross-group angular distances, averaging the central two values when even. This report does not infer a score-level cause from memberships alone.

Read all 237 original vector masks, containing 605 fragments, and the complete originals of N013/N022/N024/N026/N033/N048/N054/N055/N059/N062/N064/N080/N091/N100. All 605 fragments were checked against their original UTF-8 byte ranges. Also read complete N010/N016/N043/N066/N092 originals where counterpart masks needed context. Only visible note text was used; linked article or image contents were not assumed, and vector-map subject descriptions were not semantic evidence.

Every relation was searched across relevant alternative masks. Missing duplicate masks is not a discovery failure. Opposed claims, differing preparation methods, and defensible broad commonalities are allowed. Unrelated masks from the same note are not replacements.

An **original ID** means a V-number, including aliases; **canonical vectors** count merged alias groups once. A raw internal node is retained by the unchanged display projection only if it contains at least two notes and its note set differs from its immediate parent's; the root is retained. Accordingly, a raw useful group can have a larger displayed counterpart containing additional masks from the same notes. The reconstruction gives the production-confirmed 500 displayed M05 groups. That count is not a semantic score.

Assessment: M05 loses M01's especially focused future-lunch extension, but **total loss of the relevant three-note discovery is not established**. The broader 1517 route is real and less focused. ## Limits and audit trail

Complete small-group contents were read for M05 1031, 1367, 1369, 1425, 1450, 1502, 1506, 1510, 1517, 1575, 1599, 1629, 1665, 1703, and 1713. Internal transition sequences were examined for the Christmas, art, photography-work, raw-meat/taste, slaughter, preservation and food/emotion subtrees. Full target-note alternatives were checked before loss claims; large ancestors were searched structurally and their relevant target masks inspected, not fully semantically audited.

These cases establish specific preservation, separation and qualified alternative routes. They do not establish an overall winner, a semantic pass rate, a complete regression inventory, or a causal explanation that median is or is not robust to outliers.

Only this report was written. No code, labels, source notes, masks, vectors, models, tests, Cargo operations, commits, cards, or existing reports were changed by this inspection.
