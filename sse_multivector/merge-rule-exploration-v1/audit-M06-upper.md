# M06 connecting parents

The complete M06 hierarchy has now been inspected bottom-up. This is reading coverage, not a semantic pass rate or approval of every group. No titles, masks, memberships, or display rules were changed.

## Coverage

[Audit A](audit-M06-complete-A.md) covers 361 canonical vectors, 373 original IDs, 985 fragments and 336 merges. [Audit B](audit-M06-complete-B.md) covers another 405 canonical vectors, 416 original IDs, 1,078 fragments and 381 merges. Their roots are disjoint.

This pass reread the remaining 42 disjoint boundary groups: 150 canonical vectors, 156 original IDs, 427 fragments and 108 internal merges. The 90 remaining internal nodes connect those 91 reading roots. Their exact child pairs are recorded below and were inspected child-before-parent. Together these scopes exhaust all 916 canonical vectors, 945 original IDs, 2,490 fragments and 915 merges: 599 exact recursive M04 matches and 316 changed transitions. Original text was reopened for mental fog, coffee rituals, the island post, life advice, the bread/tea analogy, the art diary, and the kimchi joke. The four notes outside the A/B scopes—N004, N014, N042 and N094—were also read in full.

## Findings

**Useful smaller groups coexist with weak attachments.** The tea group at1561 joins the coffee material at1627, while its three-note tea child remains available. Physical activity at1736 and Christmas at1704 also remain independently available. These are not invalidated merely because their later ancestors contain other subjects. The separate [relation audit](audit-M06-relations.md) and [new-group audit](audit-M06-new-groups.md) check relevant alternate masks before making comparison claims.

**The upper tree does not establish one precise subject for every parent.** For example, the coffee chain adds the standalone dog emoji V725 at1005. Its original note asks for coffee, but that does not make the emoji itself a coffee mask. The eventual food-and-drink unions also inherit weak pairs, such as second coffee versus two pieces of bread. A plausible broad parent does not repair those smaller mismatches.

**The 99-note child is a limited narrowing step, not proof of semantic failure.** Displayed1812 contains 658 canonical vectors from every note except N018. The root contains all100 notes. This corpus was deliberately concentrated around food and daily life, so a broad parent containing most notes is unsurprising. Its size alone cannot establish incoherence. It does show that this transition removes only one note, while the finer children do most of the discovery work. Display simplification remained fixed.

The next investigation should not simply add another aggregation statistic. All six implemented rules assign the same distance to an eligible singleton pair; changed early pairings can result from earlier group availability rather than a changed pair score. The remaining question is whether a literature-supported criterion can use surrounding relationships to distinguish such connections while preserving the frozen inputs. That is a research question, not an already selected seventh rule.
