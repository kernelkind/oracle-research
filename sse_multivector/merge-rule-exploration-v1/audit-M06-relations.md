# M06: bounded recheck of nine concrete relations

M06 recovers the useful Christmas, five-note animal-flesh, and three-note tea relations absent from compact M05 groups. Food/emotion remains available in a focused group. M05's three-note restaurant-availability extension is not retained, and the photography extension remains separated. Slaughter, food over time, and preservation need qualifications: meaningful relevant access remains, but the surrounding masks and notes change.

These are provisional analyst judgments, not user approvals, a complete semantic audit, or a ranking of merge rules.

Read `M06.json`, `M01.json`, `M04.json`, `M05.json`, the baseline `../real-note-fine-segmentation-v1/hdbv3-cross-note-edges-v1/response.json`, and the frozen `vectors/vector-map.json` and `reviewed-input.json` under that input directory. End memberships alone do not establish which score comparison caused a result; implementation and fixed-state numerical verification are outside this report.

Semantic evidence is the selected fragments and original notes, not vector-map generated subject descriptions. Linked articles, threads, photographs, and videos were not opened or assumed to contain anything beyond the visible notes.

Inspected every exact child transition beneath ten M06 reading roots: **1496, 1561, 1584, 1621, 1623, 1647, 1681, 1704, 1772**, and **1489**. Large cross-run ancestors were searched structurally for the declared alternatives, not completely reread as semantic groups. This is nine named relation checks, not an audit of every M06 subtree or every possible relation.

An original ID is a V-number; aliases share one canonical leaf. The unchanged note projection keeps the root and raw internal nodes with at least two notes whose note set differs from their immediate raw parent's. Equal-note parents can therefore replace raw nodes in the display while retaining all their masks and full-note access. Recursive hiding of masks in the interface does not remove node membership. Reconstruction yields HDBv3 246, M01 428, M04 485, M05 500, and M06 488 displayed groups; these counts are not semantic scores or independent interactive verification.

## Limits

This pass establishes the named recoveries, separations, preserved partial groups and qualified alternatives. It does not judge all915 merges, endorse every upper ancestor, measure a semantic pass rate, or explain a result from depth/inversions/display counts. Coarse, clipped, compound, and tag-only masks constrain what the fixed embeddings can express; disagreement with a placement is not evidence that note-pair weighting alone caused the weakness. No new relation outside these nine families is claimed as an overall M06 advantage.

Only this report was written. No code, input, masks, vectors, generated titles, labels, inference, tests, Cargo operations, commits, cards, or other reports were changed by this inspection.
