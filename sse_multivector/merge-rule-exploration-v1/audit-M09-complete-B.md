## Scope and evidence

This report covers the 31 assigned disjoint inspection roots, not a semantic cut and not the whole M09 hierarchy. All 31 roots are displayed under the current note-set projection. Their connecting ancestors and the other inspection forest belong to the main audit.

The M09 definition in `internal-variation-and-coverage.md` was read before inspection. M09 takes the maximum of the finite, eligible nearest-partner distances from both directions. Unsupported endpoints are omitted, not certified as covered. Its distance values, possible reversals, and subtree sizes are not semantic-quality scores. This report inspects the saved result; it does not re-run or approve the implementation.

The 14 additional original IDs beyond the 450 canonical leaves were not discarded: all alias records were separately checked. In this particular forest their fragment arrays happen to agree within each canonical alias group; that was checked, not assumed.

## Findings and registry conventions

Concrete useful displayed children include the tomato family at 1323D; coffee-shop music at 1347D; the opposed slaughter/use relation at 1596D; the Christmas relation at 1512D; and mother/child food provision at 1502D. Broader connections such as food quantities, taste preferences, cooking equipment, and moral attitudes can also be legitimate. These are local evidence-backed observations, not a ranking of M09 or claims of new note-access gains.

Some joins remain weak: 1685D connects human new-food resistance to squirrels abandoning tomato attacks; 1242D mixes metaphorical mental fog with physical haze; 1117D attaches a dog-only mask to coffee. The first is an uncertain grouping interpretation; the second requires original-context disambiguation; the third lacks coffee in that selected mask. Another relevant mask may give the same note a useful route elsewhere. This bounded forest does not establish note-access losses, require all variants to reunite, or replace the main audit's generous alternate-mask relation comparisons. The original authors' dietary and physiological assertions remain attributed claims, not health advice or validated facts.

Each section gives the exact raw children, counts, alias-complete canonical leaf bindings, and **every** nested transition in increasing creation-ID order. The adjacency registry preserves the full recursive structure; it is not a flattened substitute for inspecting it.

- `leaf=Nxxx:Vxxx,Vyyy` binds a canonical leaf to every original alias and source note; exact fragment arrays and UTF-8 spans are keyed by these IDs in the frozen map.
- `idD=(left,right)` is a displayed M09 internal node.
- `idR=(left,right) >ancestorD` is raw-only and names its first displayed ancestor.
- `~M04/id,M06/id,M07/id,M08/id` records exact recursive identity in those earlier raw trees, not a semantic score.
- All unprefixed node IDs are M09 IDs. Each section root is displayed.
- The projection keeps the overall root and internal multi-note nodes whose full note set differs from their immediate raw parent's. Suppressing an equal-note-set node does not remove its complete membership. Recursively hiding parent masks already displayed below also does not change that full membership.
- Same-note-only additions and repeated aliases do not supply independent evidence merely by increasing the number of vectors.
