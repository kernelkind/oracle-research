# M07 complete subtree audit A

## Scope

This report covers exactly the 38 assigned disjoint raw reading partitions, not a proposed cut, a complete hierarchy, or the 79 connecting parents owned by the main audit. All **455 canonical vectors, 470 original-ID masks, 1,238 exact fragments, 91 complete source originals, and 417 internal transitions** inside these partitions were read. Every fragment was checked against its original UTF-8 byte span, including every alias mask; there were no mismatches or fragment/span length mismatches.

Exact recursive signatures match **84 internal transitions** to M04 or M06, within **66 maximal reusable subtrees**. A signature includes the complete original alias set at each leaf and recursively unordered child signatures. Equal top memberships alone are not reusable. All matching masks and transitions were reread here; the match registry does not imply that an inherited weak link becomes sound. The remaining **333 changed transitions** are individually classified in the registry below.

M07 uses the declared PARIS score on the frozen linear affinities, including original degree mass and absent excluded edges. This audit does not attribute a semantic effect to degree normalization alone: the definition also changes aggregation of absent connections relative to M04. No new distances, masks, model inference, candidate runs, labels, cuts or algorithms were created. Original source claims are described, not medically validated. No linked page, image, video, generated subject/title or URL string was interpreted as semantic evidence.

The main result is mixed within this assigned scope. Clear changed descendants include /1276's physical activity, /1547's morning/sleep routines, /1063's avocado-containing foods, /1426's tea, /1416's raw dairy and /1133's coffee-taste motivations. Many broader food, equipment, dietary-belief and meal-provision parents are defensible without a single shared technique. Other parents join time counts, generic evaluative language, bare tags or unrelated selected fragments too weakly. Those weak additions do not erase coherent children. This report makes no exact-vector-reunion claim about note-discovery gain or loss; that would require the separate alternate-mask comparison across complete runs.
