# M06 bounded audit of new small memberships

## Scope and result

This is a fresh-candidate audit, not a complete-tree verdict or a proposed cut. M06 changes M04's weighting of the same eligible distances: it averages within each nonempty unordered source-note-pair bin, then equally averages those bin means. It does not give every source note equal influence, remove representation choices within a bin, or cluster whole notes.

The structural screen found **155 M06 internal memberships absent from M04** with at most 12 canonical vectors and at least two notes. Membership comparison includes all original alias IDs. Preview screening is not semantic inspection of all 155. I selected the 15 disjoint roots below and read their complete masks, all nested merge transitions bottom-up, and all 44 source originals. This covers **106 canonical vectors, 110 original IDs, 320 exact fragments, and 91 internal merges**. All 320 fragments match their original UTF-8 byte spans. Of these internal merges, 51 have an exact recursive M04 match and 40 do not; I read the matching children too. Reuse requires matching aliases at leaves and matching unordered recursive child structure, not just equal memberships.

The strongest candidate useful intermediate connections are M06/1736, /1442, /1397, and /1285. Their advantages survive permissive, source-supported alternate-mask searches in M04. M06/1746 is a defensible broader cooking/eating-equipment connection; /1568 and /1514 are qualified, broader possibilities. Conversely, /1601 creates a weak spatial pair, and /1665 attaches a cold-morning observation to cooking-duration material. Those weaknesses do not erase their coherent descendants or establish that another note relationship disappeared.

Several apparent improvements are **not new note discoveries**. Butter/tallow already has M04/1355; the Paraguay–Argentina–Christmas-cost trio already has M04/1673; the three venue notes already meet in M04/1721. M06 can change mask reunion and intermediate scope without discovering a previously inaccessible note combination.

The nine separately audited relations—Christmas, photography, food over time, animal flesh, slaughter, food/emotion, preservation, restaurant availability involving N010/N050/N057, and tea—are not re-evaluated here. Shared source notes do appear in different relationships below.

At /1397, the cross-child bins are N046–N063: four edges, mean 1.207372085; N046–N096: two edges, mean 0.992212565; and N063–N096: one edge, mean 0.908021223. M04 weights those means 4:2:1; M06 weights them 1:1:1. Same-note N063 edges remain excluded. Both children are exact recursive M04 subtrees, so this is an especially concrete example of changed weighting supporting a different intermediate union without needing new masks or geometry.

The other rows prevent an overly simple causal story. A beneficial new branch need not get a lower score in isolation; an objectionable singleton pair cannot be caused by changing that pair's own formula. Earlier choices change which groups remain available. These calculations do not identify every competing edge at the greedy decision, and they do not prove a semantic mechanism from numerical proximity.

The prior M05 caveat remains important: **two masks of one note can constitute a median majority**. M06 tests a different weighting unit; it is not an independent-note-consensus rule. A source note incident to more eligible unordered note-pair bins still participates more often, and each bin's mean still depends on its actual represented vectors. Neither the gains nor the weak attachments justify claiming the representation-weight problem is solved.
