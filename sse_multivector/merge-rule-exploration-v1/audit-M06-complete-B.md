## Limits of the comparison

The complete mask scope, rather than a favorable example or a generated label, controls each judgment. Opposed views and multi-part masks can support useful broad parents; a link to one clause does not mean every other clause becomes a narrow refinement. Source context disambiguates fragments without changing what the encoded mask actually contains.

The changed/reused counts below measure inspection coverage only. They are not semantic pass rates, new-discovery counts, or an overall ranking of M06 against M04. This report does not make a global note-access comparison and does not inspect connecting ancestors above its assigned roots.
