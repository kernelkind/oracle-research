# M05 complete-subtree audit B

Provisional analyst judgments, not user-approved subjects, generated titles, a proposed cut, or a semantic pass rate. The 35 assigned roots are disjoint reading batches; their size boundary is not a quality criterion. Connecting ancestors are inspected separately by the main audit.

## Coverage and reuse

This batch contains 506 canonical vectors, 517 original IDs, 85 source notes, 1,442 exact fragments, and 471 internal merges. Both reviewers read every original vector's fragment array in their respective batches, including aliases and masks inside reused subtrees. All 1,442 fragments match their original UTF-8 byte ranges. Full originals were consulted where the selected masks required context; linked media and external pages were not imported as evidence.

The transition audit reuses 286 internal merges within 80 maximal exact recursive M04 subtree matches and inspects the remaining 185 transitions bottom-up. Reuse means equality of the complete recursive structure: a leaf's full original-ID alias set, or an unordered pair of recursively equal children. Matching only a root's members is insufficient. Merge IDs, scores, and left/right ordering are not semantic identity. The M04 and M05 canonical IDs, aliases, and four frozen input records agree exactly. The registry below identifies every reused subtree and every newly inspected merge.

The prior M04 [A](audit-M04-A.md), [B](audit-M04-B.md), [C](audit-M04-C.md), and [upper-parent](audit-M04-upper.md) reports cover the reused structure. Earlier M05 inspection is not used to excuse a changed transition: the assigned masks and changed transitions were read again here. Source evidence is the frozen `vector-map.json` fragment arrays and `reviewed-input.json` originals under `../real-note-fine-segmentation-v1/`, not the generated subject fields.

The reviewers additionally opened 44 complete original notes during this pass: N001,N002,N007,N008,N010,N012,N015,N016,N024,N027,N029,N031,N033,N037,N039,N047,N049,N051,N053,N055,N056,N058,N059,N061,N062,N063,N064,N068,N069,N070,N074,N076,N077,N080,N083,N085,N086,N088,N089,N090,N093,N094,N095,N100.
