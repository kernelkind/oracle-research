# M04 upper-parent inspection

This completes the bottom-up M04 inspection. Reports [A](audit-M04-A.md), [B](audit-M04-B.md), and [C](audit-M04-C.md) cover every exact mask and 850 internal merges in disjoint subtrees. This report inspects the remaining 65 merges connecting those subtrees. Together they cover all 916 canonical vectors, 945 original vector IDs, 2,490 fragments, and 915 merges. Counts describe inspection coverage, not a semantic pass rate.

The boundary of 32 vectors was used only to divide reading work. It did not change the hierarchy, select a cut, or supply a semantic criterion. No node titles were generated. The full original notes were consulted where the lower reports identify context-dependent meanings.

## Consequence for the investigation

M04 contains useful broad-to-narrow structure beyond the initially motivating coffee example. It also preserves weak early pairs and large, poorly informative upper transitions. M01 supplies several of the same useful note relationships and may preserve others that M04 loses. The next comparison must therefore examine both gains and regressions at the level of relevant masks and source notes. Neither raw depth nor this exhaustive inspection establishes a winner.
