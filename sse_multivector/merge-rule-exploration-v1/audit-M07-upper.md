# M07 connecting parents

M07 contains useful lower groups, but its upper structure is not a demonstrated replacement for the hierarchy. Some successive unions have recognizable broader meaning; others accumulate weakly related masks. A useful child remains useful even when its parent is questionable.

## What was inspected

The two complete-subtree audits cover 76 disjoint reading roots and 910 canonical vectors: [A](audit-M07-complete-A.md) and [B](audit-M07-complete-B.md). Four remaining reading roots contain six vectors: the singletons 0 and 871, and the two-point groups 990 and 1033. These boundaries allocate inspection work; they are not a proposed hierarchy cut.

The main inspection read all 945 original-ID masks at these 80 boundaries, including distinct fragment arrays belonging to aliases, then followed all 79 connecting parents bottom-up. The registry below records every one. The relevant full originals were checked, and the two subtree audits independently read complete originals throughout their assignments. No titles, linked images or linked articles supplied semantic evidence.

Together, the 417 + 417 subtree transitions, two remaining pair transitions, and 79 connecting transitions exhaust all 915 merges. Reading coverage is not a semantic approval rate. The [relation audit](audit-M07-relations.md) separately searches all declared relevant alternate masks before making note-discovery comparisons.

## 1. A broader parent is not automatically a misplaced child

**Evidence.** Group 1590 has 19 notes represented predominantly by coffee masks. Its children preserve bean grinding/roasting, coffee-shop experiences, coffee requests and caffeine-related material. Group 1599 adds the three-note tea child 1426. Group 1611 adds the three-note raw-dairy child 1416:

- V670 · N072: ⟦every Southerner has a different way of making sweet tea⟧
- V333 · N042: ⟦I want⟧ ⟦hot tea too⟧
- V334 · N042, an alias of V333 with different boundaries: ⟦I⟧ ⟦want⟧ ⟦hot tea⟧ ⟦too⟧
- V731 · N080: ⟦morning⟧ ⟦tea⟧ ⟦wee⟧ ⟦in that order⟧
- V504 · N060: ⟦Made⟧ ⟦coffee with raw cream⟧ ⟦today⟧
- V044 · N006: ⟦raw milk slush⟧
- V407 · N051: ⟦stack⟧ ⟦raw milk⟧

**Analysis.** A broader drinks/dairy grouping can legitimately contain coffee, tea and raw milk. Nothing in the unnamed tree says that 1599 or 1611 means coffee alone. This is therefore not the original alleged coffee-parent placement failure. The tea and raw-dairy groups remain separate children. N072 explicitly uses sweet tea as an analogy for bread-recipe variation; that does not remove its tea meaning.

There are qualifications inside the coffee side. V725 selects only a dog emoji; N079's full original makes its coffee-request context clear. V270/N034 selects caffeine, and even its full original never names coffee. Caffeine has a plausible broader connection, but it is not explicit coffee evidence. This is not evidence that all 19 notes independently discuss the same coffee activity.

## 2. Some small weak links persist into much larger parents

**Evidence.** Group 1469 contains these complete masks:

- V016 · N002: ⟦Sorrento's Savory Stroll⟧ ⟦3 delightful stops⟧
- V203 · N026: ⟦acquired 3 rotisserie chickens⟧ ⟦marked down⟧

- V305 · N039: ⟦storms⟧ ⟦Third round⟧ ⟦scheduled for⟧ ⟦fireworks⟧
- V787 · N085: ⟦Brisket⟧ ⟦on⟧ ⟦smoker⟧ ⟦about 3 hours⟧
- V592 · N069: ⟦stop eating 3 hours before bed time⟧
- V327 and V328 · N041, identical fragments: ⟦Usually I'm up like three times⟧

N041's original passage says: “It might be just a good sleep, but I've had a very good sleep last days. Usually I'm up like three times, now I just sleep like a bear.” N058 says: “Woke up to 35 degress today---brrr. It'll warm up, but fall is definitely here.” N026's discounted chickens are part of a food-preparation list. N002 advertises three stops on a food walk. Neither original turns these counts into the same activity.

Group 1723 joins 1469 with the 16-note timing/work/consumption group 1690; 1742 adds 1663's baby age, half-life, temperature and cooking-duration masks.

**Analysis.** Timing and quantities are real commonalities across some members, but the complete selection also mixes numbers of objects, repeated awakenings and temperature. A shared numeral is not by itself evidence for a useful common subject. The exact cause is not isolated: these vectors and masks already encode that numerical material, and this merge rule chooses their unions. It would be unjustified to attribute all of it to PARIS, or to infer that the sound consumption and exercise children have disappeared.

## 3. A broadly food-related parent can still contain a specific unsupported attachment

**Evidence.** Group 1659 contains V387 · N048: ⟦Linux⟧. Its first pair is 1195 with V049 · N007: ⟦I⟧ ⟦dinner⟧ ⟦Lamb⟧. The larger group adds beef, ground beef, meal sides, lemon in risotto and the Lunchly complaint. Group 1800 then joins it to the larger meal/food-provision group 1763.

N048's complete original is:

> #Come in! We will be happy: https://linuxtosha.ru/
> “How can we hope that peace and prosperity will reign on earth if our bodies are living graves in which killed animals are buried?” Lev Nikolaevich Tolstoy
> https://m.primal.net/IADr.png
> #art #alby #bitcoin #cat #Coffee #dog #dream #Gentoo #grownostr #Linux #memes #Nature #nostr #Photography #pleb #plebchain #zap #zapathon

**Analysis.** The full note has genuinely relevant animal-food content, and other masks provide legitimate access to it. That does not make the Linux mask an animal-food refinement. The distinction matters: this is a weak selected-mask attachment, not a claim that N048 is an irrelevant note everywhere or that merging algorithms should erase it. The frozen experiment cannot decide whether representation context, sparse Linux coverage or the merge criterion is principally responsible.

## 4. The two root children are not two clearly separated discovery choices

**Evidence.** Root 1830 has children 1828 (81 notes, 461 canonical vectors) and 1829 (86 notes, 455 canonical vectors). Their note sets overlap in 67 notes. The 1828 side includes meals, ingredients, animal-food preparation and food evaluations; 1829 includes coffee/tea, breads, timing, social exchanges, platform tags and household equipment. Both contain food and non-food fragments, including already documented weak attachments.

**Analysis.** Overlap is allowed and is not itself a failure. Nor must the non-semantic root have one subject. But the raw near-halving of vectors is not evidence for useful top-level choices. Each child has meaningful lower descendants alongside mixed material; neither complete upper subtree has been established as a coherent semantic alternative. M07 therefore does not solve hierarchy construction merely because its raw maximum depth is 26 rather than HDBv3's 565.

## 5. The unchanged display exposes many more groups

M07 retains 904 display groups, maximum depth 20, and 277 groups with one displayed child. Every run uses the same note-set projection. M06 retained 488 groups, depth 22, and 129 one-child groups. M07's score inversions are zero; neither monotonic scores nor fewer raw levels constitute semantic success.

The existing viewer was checked live at 1626: expand 1312, open V777's original with the two inline fragments, return, and move upward to 1718. Unopened children 1133 and 1611 appear above the still-expanded 1626/1312 chain. The view does not flatten the opened descendants or duplicate parent masks from recursively represented child notes. This verifies the inspection command, not the group's validity.

## Conclusion

The strongest evidence is local and comparative: M07 restores some useful complete extensions and loses others while retaining many small useful descendants. Its top-level note choices remain unqualified, and the unchanged display exposes substantial additional detail. The next question should concern the score's actual mechanism and observed tradeoffs, not merely reducing its tree depth or assigning convincing titles.
