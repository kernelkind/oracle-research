# M04 semantic audit A

These are observations and provisional judgments, not user-approved subjects, titles, cuts, or changes. The assigned raw roots are inspection batches, not a proposed semantic boundary. A weak attachment does not invalidate its useful descendants. Discovery means finding the relevant notes through a meaningful branch; it does not require collecting every overlapping vector from those notes.

## Evidence and exact coverage

Read every expanded vector's exact `fragments` and every internal merge under all 22 assigned raw M04 roots. Coverage is **387 canonical leaves, 403 expanded vector IDs, 946 fragments, 365 internal merges, and 55 distinct notes**. The roots are disjoint. All 403 vectors' fragments were also checked against their original-note UTF-8 `byte_spans`; all matched. Alias expansion is included in the vector/fragment counts.

Meaning evidence was the frozen vector map's fragments, original note text, and tree membership, not generated subject strings or subject titles. Original notes were read to resolve ambiguous fragments, including N003, N024, N039, N041, N043, N048, N050, N052, N053, N054, N061, N065, N068, N069, N070, N071, N072, N074, N080, N090, N092, and N098. ## Provisional synthesis

This portion contains meaningful broad-to-narrow note discovery, including strong common-tag, exercise, family/feeding, sleep/caffeine, kitchen-equipment and animal-food descendants. The N013/N048/N055 animal-slaughter connection is especially worth preserving as a broad commonality across different attitudes. Several large M04 branches mainly collect more masks from notes M01 already discovers; those are not proven gains.

The most concrete limitations are short or context-stripped masks: mental `Fog` versus weather, `Rise` without coffee, the Rome proverb treated as geography, chicken `tractor` versus transport, differing uses of five, generic waiting, and isolated names/tags. Original text can disambiguate them for this audit, but the frozen encoded mask did not always contain that disambiguation. A hierarchy-only judgment must distinguish these input limits from the merge rule's subsequent choices.

There is no basis here for making the 22 inspection roots a cut, discarding mixed parents wholesale, or demanding vector-complete versions of otherwise adequate note discoveries. The remaining upper-tree integration belongs to the main audit; this report does not claim it has audited ancestors above the assigned roots.
