Current follow-up: masks must preserve each supplied subject's exact level of generality in the local hierarchy generated by [ASv6.48](../subject-prompt-goal-v1/asv6.48/ASv6.48.md). A mask for a general heading must not inherit details belonging only to a narrower heading. Select all source wording expressing all or part of the subject at that level, including repetitions, while excluding extra meaning.

Approved empty-mask behavior: return an empty mask (`[]` through MPv3.27; `{"mask": []}` in MPv3.28) when no combination of exact source fragments can express the supplied subject at its level of generality. This does not by itself invalidate the subject: a post may support a general heading through more specific examples without providing an exact-level mask. Preserve the subject entry and its empty result. HRv1's batch adapter retains valid empty results and its encoder handoff generates no vector for them; this is tested experiment tooling, not a production rollout. Invalid responses are errors, never treated as empty masks.

## Scope

The original MPv3.5 goal covered mask-prompt development only: no model comparison, new subject generation, production change, SSE encoding or segmentation. Later separately authorized follow-ups, including diagnostic SSE comparisons, are recorded above; they do not authorize further inference or a hierarchy rebuild. No overall attempt/token cap; choose each next batch for its expected information gain. User review uses the existing tool only when consequential semantic questions need input.

The task-specific evidence and review design follows [OpenAI evaluation guidance](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/evaluation-best-practices). The particular sample sizes and acceptance tolerance are our approved experimental choices, not numbers prescribed by that guidance.
