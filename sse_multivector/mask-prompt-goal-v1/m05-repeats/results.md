The unchanged prompt therefore fails the agreed repeatability check. Eleven other subjects have accepted first masks, but the three M05 errors also make 35/36 development impossible. This does not mean masking generally failed: the observed problem remains confined to this one fixed subject.

Stop expanding this version. Investigate a small general scope-instruction change, keeping the eleven accepted examples as regression checks. Neither the subject nor its earlier judgments will be rewritten to make the candidate pass.
