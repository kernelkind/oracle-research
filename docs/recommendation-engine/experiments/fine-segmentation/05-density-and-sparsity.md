# Experiment 5: merged into Experiment 2

Status: **retired before execution**.

Unequal subject support and sparsity are now varied inside
[Experiment 2](02-nearby-subject-resolution.md). Experiment 2 encodes one
frozen pool of 96 vectors; its balanced and unequal-support conditions select
different subsets from that same pool. They do not contain the same exact
vectors. This removes the artificial boundary between two parts of one
question:

> How are nearby subjects organized under balanced support, and what changes
> when that support becomes unequal or sparse?
